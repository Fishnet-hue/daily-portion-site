DAILY PORTION

Privacy policy

Last updated August 23, 2026

We don’t collect your data

Daily Portion has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no servers of ours. Nothing is sent to us.

What stays on your device

  • Which days you answered, what you picked, and whether it was correct
  • Your streak
  • Reminder on/off and the time you chose (only if you turn reminders on)
  • A small snapshot of streak / “waiting” for the Home Screen widget

That data lives on the iPhone or iPad. Delete the app and it goes with it.

Notifications

Optional. Local only. We never send push from a server. If you’ve already answered today, we don’t remind you.

Sharing

If you tap Share, iOS opens the share sheet. We don’t see who you send it to. The message is date, correct/incorrect, and streak — not the question or verse.

Kids

We don’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date on this page.
